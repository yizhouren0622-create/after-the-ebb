## 最终使用的提示词

### 海岸

Make a legibility and color adjustment to this exact original survival game environment, retaining all composition, figures, derelict ferry, lighthouse, chalk/charcoal handmade marks, immense empty seabed. User wants it noticeably brighter and with MORE HUE. Sky is pale muted steel-blue and cold gray, overcast winter daytime; atmospheric far horizon visibly pale dusty cyan. Exposed seabed has desaturated slate-blue shadows, abandoned ferry faded cold blue paint and subtle oxidized reddish-brown rust. Brighten midground and foreground to mid-gray so the paths, ruins, and travelers are all immediately legible; do not crush blacks. Maintain ominous heavy clouds and bleak abandoned world, no cheerful sunshine, no pastel cute aesthetic. Desaturated cold blue-gray color illustration with restrained rust accents, readable pale chalk edges. 16:9 wide, no lettering, no UI, no watermark.

### 人物最终编辑

Edit the two existing traveler character sprites, preserving their poses, clothes, proportions, ages, gender, red scarf, full body framing and true transparent background. The characters are too dark and disappear in-game. Redraw all their clothing and bodies in MUCH LIGHTER chalk grays, with strong soft WHITE CHALK broken outlines around head, shoulders, coat sleeves, backpack, legs, boots. Give broad pale gray rough chalk shading to the coats and trousers, so figures are 65-75% brightness overall and stand out on charcoal backgrounds. Preserve the blurry rough simple chalk drawing quality, avoid photorealistic detail and avoid shiny anime. Faces should be pale smudged ovals with faint vague marks, not black holes. The scarf should be a clearly readable muted burgundy red. No environmental backdrop, no floor rectangle, true transparent alpha. No text. Optimize specifically for legibility as 300px tall sprites in a dark survival game.

### 街区

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original survival PC game environment background, wide 16:9. A deserted coastal neighborhood after the sea has inexplicably vanished: narrow empty street, a small shuttered grocery, old clinic entrance, windblown paper, sea salt whitening the road, cracked windowpanes and hanging electrical cables, distant apartment blocks fading in white fog. Beautiful fine graphite and black ink crosshatching on warm gray paper, sparse watercolor wash, hand-drawn indie graphic novel scenery, atmospheric environmental storytelling, bone-white and charcoal palette with one tiny faded rust-red cloth tied to a railing, solemn haunting quiet, delicate detailed architecture. Eye-level cinematic wide shot, no human figures, no text or readable signs, no interface, no border, no watermark. Original composition.

### 灯塔

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide 16:9 original survival adventure environment art. A lonely lighthouse with a weathered radio relay annex built on a rocky island now stranded high above an empty seabed. Close enough to see iron stairs, rusted aerials, exposed pipes and a circular signal horn; paths of wind-eroded salt lead to its door, a faint pale shaft of light from overcast sky, no sea visible. Fine black ink and graphite crosshatching with desaturated watercolor washes on warm gray old paper, solemn beautiful indie graphic novel style, hand-drawn 2D, elegant depth and environmental details, charcoal and bone-white palette with a tiny faded rust-red warning pennant. Composition lighthouse right of center, huge sky upper left. No humans, no lettering, no readable signs, no UI, no border, no watermark. Entirely original composition.

街区与灯塔在引擎内加入冷色场景照明。界面和图标由程序绘制；声音为本地合成，未使用外部音乐。
